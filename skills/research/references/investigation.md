# Investigation

Produce one accessible report answering the brief. Stay within authorised source reads and report writes. Perform this assignment directly.

## Method

1. **Frame the investigation.** Identify every material question and claim needed to cover the scope. A claim is material when its accuracy could change the answer, recommendation, or confidence. Resolve discoverable facts directly. Return necessary user-held questions to the caller before dependent work.
2. **Trace and appraise evidence.** Use **citation chaining** to seek the original evidence for every material factual claim and attempt verification in its full context. Read the source rather than rely on search snippets. Use **lateral reading** to check relevant expertise and credibility beyond the source's own account. Assess evidence provenance, methods, currency, applicability, completeness, and interests for the particular claim. Primary status or publisher reputation alone does not establish reliability.
3. Permit secondary analysis or synthesis when that appraisal supports its use. Verify material underlying claims where possible. If an original remains inaccessible after reasonable alternative access attempts, cite the account actually read, disclose the verification limit, and qualify the conclusion. Assess a synthesis's methods and limitations without claiming to have audited every underlying study. Official documentation establishes an organisation's published contract or claim. Comparative effectiveness needs suitable independent evidence. For example, a vendor's “fastest” claim establishes its advertising, not measured superiority.
4. **Challenge and cover.** Seek credible contrary evidence and genuinely independent evidence routes. Repeated accounts of one original do not corroborate it. Resolve discrepancies where possible. Otherwise retain the conflict and its effect on the answer. Continue while meaningful gaps remain investigable. Stop when every scoped question has an answer or an explicit unresolved finding and further searching is unlikely to materially change the result. Missing evidence is distinct from evidence against.
5. **Write the report.** Invoke [$write-prose](../../write-prose/SKILL.md) with the brief, findings, sources, and audience. Use [the report template](../assets/research-report-template.md) unless the caller supplied a format. Adapt the layout to the subject, retaining an answer-first structure, scope and research date, organised findings, material conflicts and limitations, and references. Cite material claims near their evidence, including in the summary. Identify inference and ground requested recommendations in findings. Omit empty sections.
6. **Verify and save.** Check the actual report against the sources read. Correct unsupported claims, misleading certainty, unresolved citation targets, and links that do not identify the evidence. Include relevant dates, versions, or precise locations where they affect interpretation. Save the report.

## Finish

Return only the report path and material limitations or blockers.

**Done only when** every scoped question has a supported answer or an explicit unresolved finding explaining the search and its implications, claims and references have been checked, and the report is saved. A missing prerequisite remains a blocker. Keep any partial report clearly labelled incomplete.
