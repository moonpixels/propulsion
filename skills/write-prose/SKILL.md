# Write prose

Produce natural prose that fits its reader while preserving **meaning, evidence, and required wording**.

## Inputs

Use the subject, supplied facts, purpose, audience, medium, genre, constraints, and required structure. Apply this skill to all human-facing prose, including correspondence, reports, articles, scripts, social posts, and documentation. Default to UK English and follow explicit language or voice requests. The caller owns the artefact and its factual, structural, and procedural requirements.

## Method

1. Make the point, stakes, or useful question clear early. Prefer concrete nouns, visible actions, exact details, and accurate verbs. Use plain words by default, keeping specialist terms when their precision serves the audience. Ground unfamiliar concepts before relying on them.
2. Connect claims to useful explanation, examples, evidence, comparisons, trade-offs, or consequences. Put evidence near its claim. State uncertainty and its practical effect. Never invent experience, quotations, evidence, agreement, or certainty to create a voice.
3. Give each paragraph a clear job and order the material so each part earns the next. Use lists for parallel items, numbers for sequences, and tables for comparisons. Default to literal, complete sentences with one clear main point. Keep related conditions and consequences together. Use analogy or other expressive techniques only when the audience and genre benefit. Vary pace as the thought changes, preserving useful parallelism and genre conventions.
4. Use full stops and commas for authored prose. **Do not use em dashes, semicolons, or parentheses**, or substitute another dash for an em dash. Reserve colons for lists and examples. Preserve exact quotations, technical literals, and required formatting. Use sentence-case headings, sparse emphasis, and no decorative emojis by default. Follow the destination's quotation-mark style and explicit presentation requirements.
5. Review the draft for fidelity and structure, then repair unsupported claims, vague praise, stock framing, needless jargon, repetition, and over-compression. Use the review reference for concrete patterns and repairs. Retain relevant general advice that helps the reader understand or act. Read for rhythm and correct language usage. End with the conclusion, implication, or next action the piece has earned.

## Finish

Return the requested prose in its destination or format. Check it against the brief and supplied facts. Stop when further editing would merely make it different rather than clearer, more engaging, or more faithful. Leave unresolved factual gaps visible instead of filling them with invented detail.
